Welcome

This study looks at how people remember new information. It takes about 6 minutes.

What will happen

  1. Assignment – the website randomly places you in one of three groups. You don't choose.
  2. Learning material – you receive a short piece of information about an animal in one format. It plays automatically for a fixed time ().
  3. Quiz – you answer multiple-choice questions about it straight away.
  4. Result – you see your score, and it is saved automatically.
How this study is kept fair (controlled variables): every participant gets exactly the same information (the same script, word for word, in the same parts), for exactly the same total time (, s per part, advancing automatically with no pausing or replaying), and then takes the identical quiz. The only thing that differs between groups is the format the information is presented in.

Please do the study on your own, without notes. If you are in a listening or video group you will need sound (headphones are best). Participation is voluntary; only your initials (optional) and quiz answers are recorded.
